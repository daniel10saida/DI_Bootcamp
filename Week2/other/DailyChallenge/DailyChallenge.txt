### Step 2: The Role of Data Analysis

The data analyzed included air pollution levels and the number of vehicles entering the congestion zone.

The researchers compared data from before and after the congestion pricing policy was introduced, as well as data from inside and outside the zone.

The purpose of the analysis was to understand how the policy affected traffic and pollution levels.

### Step 3: The Impact of Data Analysis

Data analysis made it possible to compare more factors, such as weather, seasons, and differences between locations.

Without it, it would have been harder to know whether the changes were caused by the new policy or by other factors.

This information can help policymakers make better decisions about how to reduce traffic congestion and pollution in the future.

### Step 4: Results and Significance

The researchers estimated that fine particulate pollution (PM2.5) inside the congestion zone decreased by approximately 13% beyond expected trends, while the number of vehicles entering the area dropped by around 11%.

These results suggest that congestion pricing can help reduce traffic and improve air quality, and show how data analysis can help evaluate whether a policy is actually working.
